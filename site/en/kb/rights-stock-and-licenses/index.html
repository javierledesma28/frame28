Rights, stock and licenses

What material you can send us, what we use, who owns what and what documentation you get with the delivery.

Summary in one sentence: your material is yours, the result is yours, and what we add has a documented license. The full terms are at frame28.app/terms; this is the practical version.

What you send us

You must be able to tick every box before sending it:

  • [ ] Your brand shot the video, or you have written permission from whoever shot it.
  • [ ] The people who appear (employees, creators, customers) have authorized their image and voice for advertising.
  • [ ] If minors appear, you have their guardians' consent.
  • [ ] The music in it is licensed for advertising, or you authorize us to remove it.
  • [ ] The logos, products and brands shown are yours or you have permission to show them.
  • [ ] The prices, discounts and figures you want on screen are real and current.
  • [ ] If it's creator or affiliate material, you have their authorization to edit and publish it under your brand.

If we spot something evident (commercial music, someone else's logo, a minor without apparent consent) we flag it before going on.

What we add

  • Supporting images and video (B-roll). Only from banks with a commercial license that requires no attribution (today, Pexels and Pixabay) or material you give us. Every element is documented in the delivery with its author, page and license. You can use them within the pieces; not extract them to resell separately, nor suggest that the people shown endorse your product.
  • Typefaces. Free-license, or yours with your license.
  • Music. Not included. If you want music, you supply it with a license or we propose a licensed library as an add-on.
  • Icons and graphics. Drawn by code or from open libraries.

Who owns what

  • The final pieces are yours upon payment: to publish wherever you want, including paid advertising, with no time or territory limit, no watermark.
  • Your material remains yours. We use it only for your order and delete it from our machines ninety days after final delivery.
  • What isn't assigned: our software, method, templates and edit scripts. With Team you get the software under an open license and your brand configuration, which is yours.

How we handle your video

It's processed on our machines; it isn't uploaded to third-party editing platforms. To direct the edit we use language models that see the transcript, the brief and review frames, under terms that don't allow training on your data. If your material contains personal data, we sign the data processing addendum.

Portfolio

We may name you as a customer and show excerpts of pieces you've already published, unless you tell us not to. If you're a Founder, the case study is part of the agreement and you approve it before it's published.
