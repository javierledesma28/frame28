Brand and brief

The ten brief questions, answered live with an example, and what we do with each answer. Eight minutes.

Video in preparation: shot to camera and edited with Frame28. The script is below in the meantime.

What you'll learn

  • Why the edit uses your words and not ours.
  • How to answer the ten questions in fifteen minutes.
  • What happens with your colors, your font and your logo.

Script outline

  1. Hook. "The sentence that sells the most in your video is already written. It's on your website."
  2. What the brief is and why only once. Answered once; the brand is configured; every piece comes out with it.
  3. Questions 1 to 5, live with an example kit brand: what you sell, hero product with price, literal promises, social proof with real figures, closing.
  4. Questions 6 to 10: colors (buttons and text), font, vector logo with a dark-background version, tone in three adjectives, what you don't want.
  5. What we do with each answer. Promises → kinetic titles in the second you say them. Product → one title, done right. Social proof → counter before the closing. Closing → card with logo, website and code.
  6. Mistakes we see. Figures rounded up; promises that aren't on the website; a small PNG logo; "use whatever color".
  7. If you have brand guidelines. Answer 1 to 5 and 10; we read the rest there.
  8. Closing. Homework and lesson four.

Key lines

"Your words, not ours." · "Once, and it comes out on its own." · "Real figures or none." · "The logo, as a vector."

What the edit puts on screen

The ten questions as a board with focus on the one being explained; an example product title with price; a social proof counter; an example closing card in the brief's colors.

Homework

Answer the ten questions for the video you chose in lesson one. Fifteen minutes. Keep it: that's your brief.
