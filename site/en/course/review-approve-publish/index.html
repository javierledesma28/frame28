Review, approve and publish

How to read the contact sheet in a minute, how to write the round of changes and what to publish where. Eight minutes.

Video in preparation: shot to camera and edited with Frame28. The script is below in the meantime.

What you'll learn

  • The seven review points, in order.
  • How to write a round of changes that lands first time.
  • Which piece goes where and with which file.

Script outline

  1. Hook. "This is a contact sheet. In one minute you know whether the video is right."
  2. What it is and how to read it. A frame every few seconds, in order; the sheet first, then only the stretches that catch your eye.
  3. The seven points. Nothing covers what matters; the text says what you say; figures, prices and names; pace; legibility; subtitles; closing.
  4. The round of changes, live. Write five example lines with minute, change and exact text.
  5. What isn't a round. Another video, another brief, new pieces, changes after approval.
  6. Approving. One email. Ten business days with no reply means approved. Keep your copy.
  7. Publishing. Long video to YouTube with the SRT and the cover; shorts to TikTok, Reels and Shorts with hook A and then hook B; 1:1 to the product page; language versions to each market.
  8. Measuring. What to look at after seven days: retention in the first three seconds and at fifteen; which hook performed. On Studio you get it in the monthly report.
  9. Closing. Homework and lesson six (Team only).

Key lines

"The sheet first, then the video." · "Minute, change, exact text." · "One email is enough." · "Hook A, then hook B."

What the edit puts on screen

The real contact sheet of this lesson; the seven points as steps; the round of changes being typed on a card; a piece → platform → file table.

Homework

With the lesson's example video, write a three-line round of changes in the minute · change · exact text format.
