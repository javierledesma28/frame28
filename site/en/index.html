How it works

Three steps. The third one is a sentence.

1 · Your Brand Memory, on frame28.app

Paste your website and two or three of your videos. Frame28 drafts a first version: colors, logo and font; your voice and the words you never use; what you sell, to whom and with what proof; your products with prices and codes. You correct it in ten minutes. From then on, it remembers.

2 · Create the campaign

Goal (launch, sell, educate), product, platforms and formats, languages, date and the source videos: a YouTube link or a file. Out comes the brief and the list of pieces you'll receive.

3 · In your Claude Code: "Edit the campaign"

The plugin signs in with your account, receives your Memory and the brief, and directs the edit: on-screen steps, the number being said, the callout where you point, the before and after, the proof, the call to action. It renders on your machine and delivers to the web, where you review and approve.

Brand Memory

What Frame28 remembers about you so every video is yours

It's the difference between a clip generator and a studio that works for your brand. Fill it in once, correct it whenever you like, and it learns with every campaign.

Brand

Colors, typography, logo variants and the pace of the animations. What is seen.

Voice

How you speak: formal or casual, short sentences, words you never use, when you shout in caps. What is read.

Story

What you are in one sentence, for whom, what you promise, what proves it and which objections need an answer.

Catalog

Products with price, discount code, link and features. What the director names and points at.

Hooks

The approved ones, the banned ones and the ones that worked. The next campaign starts from the winners.

Languages and platforms

A glossary per language for names and figures, safe zones for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, a default call to action.

What every campaign produces

From one video, the whole campaign

  • The edited long video with your brand: titles, steps, figures, callouts, before and after, product card and closing.
  • Vertical shorts (9:16) with two different hooks per segment, to rotate creatives without shooting again.
  • A YouTube cover and a 1:1 version for the product page or the marketplace listing.
  • Readable subtitles (SRT) and versions in other languages with the same timing.
  • The publishing copy: title, description, hashtags and a first comment with your call to action, per platform.
  • The storyboard of every piece, reusable: "the next one, like this but with the new product."

What it isn't. Not a captioning tool, not an automatic clip generator. You don't edit anything: you direct with a sentence. And it's honest about what it needs: Claude Code on your computer (your Claude subscription is yours and separate), plus ffmpeg and Node, which the installer sets up for you.

What's behind it

How it's built

  • Your Claude Code directs. The plugin gives it the judgment (how a video that sells is edited) and your Brand Memory (what to say and how). It uses your Claude subscription, under its terms; Frame28 never sees your conversation.
  • Your video stays on your machine. Transcription, cuts, speaker cutout and rendering happen locally. Only the pieces you deliver and your Memory go up to frame28.app. On Studio we process it on ours, under the terms.
  • Licensed material. Stock B-roll carries a per-video commercial license, documented in the delivery. Your footage is yours; nobody reuses it.
  • Your deliverables and your Memory are yours. Final files, subtitles, storyboards and an exportable Memory. No watermark, no lock-in.
  • Open engine. The code that edits your videos is public (MIT) and improves with every campaign: github.com/javierledesma28/frame28.
  • What's next. Frame28 Cloud, synthetic-voice dubbing, a connection to your analytics so the Memory learns on its own. See the full roadmap.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to use Frame28?

A frame28.app account with a plan, Claude Code on your computer (Windows or Mac; the Claude subscription is separate) and a video. The one-line installer sets up ffmpeg, Node and the plugin for you.

Do I need to know how to edit video?

No. You direct with sentences: "edit the campaign," "that title smaller," "drop short three." Frame28 decides what goes on screen and when, with your Brand Memory as the guide.

How long does a campaign take?

Hours, not days. From a 2:40 tutorial came three long videos in three languages, six shorts and four covers with two hours of machine time on a laptop. You review at the end, not in the middle.

Where is my video processed?

On your computer. Only the pieces you deliver and your Brand Memory go up to frame28.app. Frame28 Cloud, for those who'd rather install nothing, is on the roadmap.

Does it work with an already-edited video?

Yes. Frame28 works on produced video (with titles, cuts and music) and on creator clips. It detects the text already on screen so it never covers it.

What about languages?

On-screen text and subtitles are translated with your glossary and fitted to the original rhythm: the German version runs as long as the English one and the cuts land in the same place. Dubbing comes later.

What happens to my Brand Memory if I leave?

It's yours: export it as JSON along with your storyboards and deliverables. The engine is open, so you can always keep rendering.

I don't want to run anything. What then?

Studio: Think28 edits your campaigns with Frame28 on your own account. First campaign 990 USD or monthly 2,490 USD.

Can I try it for free?

Yes. Create a free account: three trial campaigns with the Think28 brand and a Frame28 end card. The plugin always signs in with your account; the rendering engine is open source (frame28.t28.io).
