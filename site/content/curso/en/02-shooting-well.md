## What you'll learn

- The ten shooting rules and why each one matters for the edit.
- The ten-second test before shooting for real.
- What to do if the video is already shot or edited.

## Script outline

1. **Hook.** "This shot is badly filmed. This one is fine. The difference is ten things and none of them cost money."
2. **Fixed camera and background.** Show text behind the speaker working; show what happens with a pan.
3. **Light from the front.** Compare sharp hands with semi-transparent hands in low light.
4. **Air on one side.** Show a title with room and one covering the face.
5. **Mic close.** Quiet audio can be fixed; background noise can't.
6. **Short sentences with a pause.** Show a clean subtitle versus one that cuts mid-idea.
7. **Slow gestures.** Point and hold: the callout appears. Quick gesture: it doesn't.
8. **Proper nouns in writing.** Example of a brand transcribed wrong and fixed.
9. **One take per chapter.** Change position between blocks.
10. **1080p at 30 fps, horizontal.** The verticals come from the horizontal.
11. **No prior editing.** What's already baked in can't be moved or translated.
12. **The ten-second test.** Shoot, watch on the phone, three questions.
13. **If you already have the video.** Voice-over tutorials, creator clips: they work too.
14. **Closing.** Homework and lesson three.

Numbered steps 1 to 10 with progress; before/after on rules 2, 3 and 6 with shots filmed badly and well on purpose;
a callout on rule 7 that only appears with the right gesture.

## Homework

Shoot the ten-second test with your phone following the rules and watch it: can you hear yourself? Is there air on
one side? Is the light even?
