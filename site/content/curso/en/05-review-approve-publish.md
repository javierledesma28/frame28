## What you'll learn

- The seven review points, in order.
- How to write a round of changes that lands first time.
- Which piece goes where and with which file.

## Script outline

1. **Hook.** "This is a contact sheet. In one minute you know whether the video is right."
2. **What it is and how to read it.** A frame every few seconds, in order; the sheet first, then only the stretches
   that catch your eye.
3. **The seven points.** Nothing covers what matters; the text says what you say; figures, prices and names; pace;
   legibility; subtitles; closing.
4. **The round of changes, live.** Write five example lines with minute, change and exact text.
5. **What isn't a round.** Another video, another brief, new pieces, changes after approval.
6. **Approving.** One email. Ten business days with no reply means approved. Keep your copy.
7. **Publishing.** Long video to YouTube with the SRT and the cover; shorts to TikTok, Reels and Shorts with hook A
   and then hook B; 1:1 to the product page; language versions to each market.
8. **Measuring.** What to look at after seven days: retention in the first three seconds and at fifteen; which
   hook performed. On Studio you get it in the monthly report.
9. **Closing.** Homework and lesson six (Team only).

## Key lines

"The sheet first, then the video." · "Minute, change, exact text." · "One email is enough." · "Hook A, then hook B."
