The hook decides in three seconds whether the viewer stays. On the best videos, 45 to 55 % of people do; on weak
ones, under 25 %. And more than 70 % watch with the sound off: the hook is **text on screen**, not just what's said.

## The ten types

| Type | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Transformation | From {before} to {after} | *From a scratched jar to a gift* |
| Risk | {Big thing} with ZERO practice (mistake?) | *Engraving a guitar with ZERO practice (mistake?)* |
| Curiosity | Can you actually {X}? | *Can you actually engrave an egg?* |
| Pain | Your {thing} is RUINING your {result} | *Your tape is RUINING your lines* |
| Benefit | The ONLY {thing} you need for {result} | *The only tutorial you need for glass* |
| Social proof | {N}+ makers can't be wrong | *{N}+ customers can't be wrong* |
| Price | {price} · everything included | *$59.99 · everything included* |
| List of three | 3 steps: {a} · {b} · {c} | *Tape · trace · engrave* |
| Personal story | I made my {person} their dream {thing} | *I made my dad his dream wallet* |
| Urgency | This week only: {offer} | only if it's true |

## Where they come from: your own video

We don't invent. We read the transcript and look for three things:

1. **The result sentence**: "and that's it", "look how it turned out", "done". It's the after of the transformation.
2. **The objection you answer**: "what if it cracks?", "isn't it too hard?". It's the pain.
3. **The literal promise**: "beginner friendly", "no guesswork", "in ten minutes". It's the benefit.

With that we propose three different hooks for the same segment, in the speaker's own words. You choose, or leave
it to us.

## Why two hooks per short

Platforms burn a creative in one or two weeks: the same people see it, stop reacting and reach drops. The cure isn't
shooting again: it's rotating the opening. That's why every short is delivered with two different hooks and the
same body. You publish one; when it drops, you publish the other.

## The rules of the on-screen hook

- Two lines at most, four or five words per line.
- It appears before half a second and leaves before the fourth.
- In vertical it goes in the top band, away from the app icons.
- On light backgrounds, an accent-colored box; on backgrounds with light, a black box.
- Never a figure that isn't on your website.

## After the hook: the arc

In a thirty-second short: hook (0–3 s), problem or promise in your literal words (3–6 s), demonstration with
numbered steps (6–20 s), result with before and after (20–24 s), social proof (24–26 s) and call to action
(26–30 s). In a long video the moments are the same, more spread out. If your video lacks one of these moments,
you'll notice it in the proposed hooks: we'll tell you what to shoot next time to have it.
