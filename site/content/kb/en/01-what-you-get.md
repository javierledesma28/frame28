## What you get from one video

Up to five different things come out of a single video. You get all of them on Launch and Studio; on Team your own
team produces them.

| Piece | What it's for | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Edited long video | your YouTube channel, the website, the email to customers | MP4 1080p, 16:9, 30 fps |
| Vertical shorts with two hooks each | TikTok, Reels, Shorts; the same body with two different openings to rotate | MP4 1080×1920 |
| Cover | the YouTube thumbnail and the post image | PNG 1920×1080 |
| 1:1 version | the product page, Amazon, square Instagram | MP4 1080×1080 |
| Subtitles | the file you upload so the platform indexes it and the 70 % who watch muted can read | SRT |
| Versions in other languages | on-screen text and subtitles translated, with the same timing | MP4 + SRT per language |

Everything carries your brand: colors, typography, logo and the closing card.

## What you have to send

1. **The video.** A link (YouTube, Vimeo, Drive, Dropbox) or the file. The original beats a re-compressed download.
   If it's a person talking to camera, read "How to shoot so it comes out right" first. If it's an already-edited
   video (a voice-over tutorial, a creator's clip), it works as is: we build on top.
2. **The brand brief.** Ten questions answered in fifteen minutes. If you have brand guidelines, attach them and
   answer only what the guidelines don't cover.
3. **The logo as a vector** (SVG or PDF). A large PNG works if there's nothing else.
4. **What you want to sell with this video**: the product, its current price and discount, the code if there is
   one, and the call to action (website, "link in bio", QR). If you don't say, we use what your website says.

That's enough to start. Without the brief and the logo, the clock doesn't start.

## How long it takes

**Launch: five business days** from the moment we have the video, the brief, the logo and the payment. On Studio
we agree on a monthly calendar: which video goes in each week and when you get it.

## What delivery looks like

You get a link with two things: the **contact sheet** (one image with frames from every piece, to see it all at a
glance) and the pieces. You have ten business days to approve or ask for your round of changes; if we don't hear
from you, it's deemed approved. The link stays active for at least sixty days: keep your copy.

## The round of changes

One list, in one go, with everything you want adjusted: on-screen text, a timing, a color, the order of blocks,
the hook you prefer among the proposed ones, a subtitle or translation fix, the cover. How to write it so it lands
first time: article "How to review the contact sheet and ask for changes".

Not a round of changes, quoted separately: a different source video, a new brief, languages or pieces that weren't
in the order, or changes after approval.

## What happens next

You publish. If on Launch you don't publish at least half of the pieces within thirty days, we refund it. And if
you want to keep going, Studio: four videos a month with a calendar and a report on which hooks performed.
