The edit doesn't decorate: it reinforces what you sell, with your words and your brand. For that we need to know
ten things. Keep it short; if a question doesn't apply, say so.

## The ten questions

1. **What do you sell and to whom?** One sentence. "Kits to engrave glass at home, for people who've never done it."
2. **What's the hero product of this video?** Exact name, current price, current discount and what's included. We
   put it on screen once, properly, when you name it or show it.
3. **What promises does your website make, literally?** Copy the sentences as they are: "everything included", "no
   experience needed", "ready in ten minutes". We use your words, not ours.
4. **What social proof can you show?** Customers, average rating, number of reviews, guarantee. Only real, current
   figures: they go on screen before the closing.
5. **What does the closing have to say?** Website, "link in bio", discount code, QR. And the final line, if you have one.
6. **Colors.** The accent (buttons), the ink (text) and the background. Hex codes if you have them; if not, we take
   them from your website and confirm them with you.
7. **Typography.** The font of your website or your guidelines. If it's a paid font and you don't have a video
   license, tell us which one and we'll find the closest free-license match.
8. **Logo.** As a vector (SVG or PDF), with the dark-background version if it exists. The logo is yours: we don't
   publish it or reuse it.
9. **Tone.** Three adjectives. "Warm, close, no jargon." Or send a video of yours you like and one you don't.
10. **What you don't want.** Words you never use, competitors not to name, colors to avoid, humor yes or no.

## What we do with this

With the answers we create your brand in Frame28 once: colors, font, logo with its variants, tagline and closing.
Every new piece comes out with it. If you change your identity, tell us and we update it.

## If you have brand guidelines

Attach them and answer only questions 1 to 5 and 10. We read the rest there.

## One tip

Question 3 changes the result the most. The sentences that work on your website work on screen: a two-word
kinetic title with your promise, in the second you say it, sells more than any effect.
