## What the contact sheet is

A single image with a frame from every piece every few seconds, in order. It lets you see in a minute what would
take twenty to play back: where each text comes in, whether something covers the face, whether the pace is right.
You get it with the pieces; review the sheet first and then only the stretches that catch your eye.

## What to look at, in this order

1. **Nothing covers what matters.** The face, the hands when they're showing something, the product when you're showing it.
2. **The text says what you say.** Each title comes in when you name it, not before or after. A number on screen
   that doesn't match the one you say is the first thing a viewer notices.
3. **Figures, prices and names.** They're yours: check them one by one. We reproduce what's in the brief and on
   your website; if it changed, tell us.
4. **Pace.** A visual event every two to four seconds; no stretch longer than eight seconds with nothing; never two
   big texts at once.
5. **Legibility.** Light text on a light background can't be read: look at the stretches with a table, a white wall or fabric.
6. **Subtitles.** Read five at random. A misspelled proper noun is fixed in seconds if you tell us.
7. **Closing.** Website, code, call to action and logo: what has to stay in the viewer's head.

## How to write the round of changes

One list, one send, with the moment and the change. Like this:

> 0:12 · the product title, with the new price ($54.99).
> 0:48 · remove the text "no experience"; it covers the bit when I show it.
> 1:30 · the subtitle says "blade"; it's "carbide blade".
> short 2 · I prefer hook B. Cover with that same hook.
> All: the logo a little smaller in the closing.

What helps: the minute and second (it's on the contact sheet), the exact text you want, and deciding between the
options we propose. What delays: "I'm not convinced by the opening" without saying what, or changes arriving in
three different emails.

## What's in and what's out

In the round: text, timing, colors, order of blocks, hook choice, subtitles, translations, cover. Not in, quoted
separately: another source video, a different brief, new languages or pieces, changes after approval. Launch
includes one round; Studio, two per piece.

## Approving

An "approved" by email is enough. If ten business days pass with no reply, it's deemed approved and we move to
final delivery. Keep your copy: the link lasts at least sixty days.
