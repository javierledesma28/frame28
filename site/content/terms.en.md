**Version 1.0 · [[publication date]]**

These terms govern the Frame28 service between **Think28** ([[legal name]], tax ID [[tax ID]], [[address]], Spain;
"Think28") and the customer who signs an order form or accepts these terms when ordering on frame28.app (the
"Customer"). The service is for businesses and professionals, not consumers.

## 1. Subject

Frame28 turns videos supplied by the Customer into video pieces edited with the Customer's brand: long video,
vertical shorts, cover, product-page version, subtitles and versions in other languages. The specific scope of each
engagement (tier, number of videos, languages, deadlines and price) is set in the **order form**, which is part of
this agreement. If they conflict, the order form prevails.

## 2. Definitions

- **Customer Material:** videos, audio, images, logos, text, data and any other content the Customer gives Think28
  for the service.
- **Brief:** the Customer's answers to the Frame28 brand questionnaire (colors, logo, tone, product, messages, call
  to action) and any written instructions.
- **Piece:** each final file delivered (a long video, a short, a cover, a 1:1 version, a subtitle file, a version in
  another language).
- **Deliverables:** all the Pieces of an order.
- **Contact sheet:** the review image with frames of every Piece that Think28 sends before delivery.
- **Round of changes:** a single list of adjustments to the delivered Pieces, sent by the Customer in one go, as
  described in clause 6.
- **Stock:** third-party video or image material under a commercial license that Think28 adds to a Piece.

## 4. Process and deadlines

1. **Order.** The Customer signs the order form or accepts these terms in the order form on the website.
2. **Materials.** The Customer delivers the Customer Material and the Brief. The delivery deadline starts on the
   business day after Think28 has everything it needs and the initial payment (clause 8).
3. **Direction and editing.** Think28 analyzes the video, decides what goes on screen and when, writes the edit
   script and generates the Pieces.
4. **Review.** Think28 sends the Contact sheet and the Pieces. The Customer has **10 business days** to approve
   them or request its Round of changes. With no answer in that period, the Pieces are deemed approved.
5. **Final delivery.** After the Round of changes, Think28 delivers the final Pieces through a download link
   available for at least 60 days. The Customer must keep its own copy.

Deadlines: Launch, 5 business days to the first delivery. Studio, per the agreed monthly calendar. If Think28
exceeds the deadline by more than 5 business days for reasons attributable to it, the Customer may terminate the
order and recover what it paid for the undelivered Pieces.

## 5. Customer obligations regarding its material

The Customer warrants that:

- It holds the **rights** needed for Think28 to use the Customer Material in the service and for the resulting
  Pieces to be exploited: copyright in the video and audio, image and voice rights of the people who appear
  (including employees, creators and customers), trademarks and logos, and any music and fonts contained in the material.
- **Creator or affiliate** material delivered for normalization comes with the creator's authorization to be
  edited and published by the brand.
- The **claims** it asks to show on screen (prices, discounts, customer figures, product properties, comparisons)
  are true and comply with advertising and consumer regulations in the markets where they will be published.
  Think28 reproduces that data; it does not verify it.
- The material does not infringe third-party rights and contains no unlawful content.

The Customer will hold Think28 harmless against third-party claims arising from a breach of these warranties. If
Think28 spots an evident problem (unlicensed commercial music, third-party logos, minors without apparent consent),
it will flag it and may suspend the order until it is resolved.

## 6. Rounds of changes and additional work

A Round of changes covers adjustments to what was delivered: on-screen text, timing, colors, order of blocks,
choice among the proposed hooks, subtitle and translation corrections, cover change.

It is not a Round of changes, and is quoted as additional work: a different source video, a new or substantially
changed Brief (another brand, product or message), languages or pieces not in the order form, or changes after
approval. Launch includes 1 Round; Studio, 2 per Piece.

## 7. Stock, fonts and third-party tools

- **Stock.** Think28 only adds Stock from providers with a commercial license that requires no attribution
  (currently Pexels and Pixabay) or material supplied by the Customer. Every Stock element is documented in the
  delivery (provider, author, license). Stock is licensed for use within the Pieces; the Customer may not extract
  and resell it separately, nor use it in a way that suggests the people shown endorse its product, per the
  provider's terms.
- **Music.** Not included unless the Customer supplies it with a license. [[decision: offer licensed library music
  as an add-on]]
- **Fonts.** Typefaces in the Pieces are free-license or supplied by the Customer with its license.
- **Tools.** The edit is generated with Think28 software (partly published as open source under the MIT license)
  and third-party tools run on Think28 machines. For direction of the edit, Think28 uses Anthropic language models
  (Claude): they receive the video transcript, the Brief, the edit script and review frames, under Anthropic's
  commercial terms, which do not allow training models on that data [[confirm current subscription terms or move
  to the API]]. Video and audio files are not uploaded to third-party editing platforms.

## 9. Launch guarantee

If the Customer does not publish at least half of the Pieces of a Launch order within **30 days** of final
delivery, it may request a full refund of the amount paid for that order, by email, before day 40. Think28 refunds
within 15 days and the Customer loses the right to use the unpublished Pieces.

The guarantee does not apply if delivery could not be completed for lack of materials, Brief or approval from the
Customer, nor to Studio, Team or add-ons.

- **Portfolio.** Think28 may name the Customer as a customer and show excerpts of already-published Pieces as

## 11. Confidentiality

Each party will treat as confidential the non-public information it receives from the other (launches,
unannounced products, figures, unpublished materials, negotiated prices) and will not use it for purposes other
than the service. The obligation lasts two years from the last delivery. Information that is already public,
already known to the party or that must be disclosed by law is not confidential.

- Think28's total liability on any ground is limited to the amount paid by the Customer in the 12 months before the

- The Customer is ultimately responsible for reviewing each Piece before publishing it: text, figures,

## 14. Term and termination

Launch ends with final delivery or the guarantee refund. Studio and Team have the term set in clause 8. Either party
may terminate if the other breaches a material obligation and fails to cure it within 15 days of written notice. On
termination, the Customer pays for the Pieces already delivered and keeps the rights in them; Think28 refunds what
was charged for undelivered Pieces.

## 15. Founders program

The first five customers who join the program receive: early-adopter pricing locked for 12 months from their first
order form on any tier, a vote on the product roadmap, their logo on frame28.app as a founding brand and a direct
line to the team. In return, the Customer commits to: a publishable case study with figures (Think28 drafts it, the
Customer approves within 10 business days), a testimonial and two 30-minute feedback sessions. The program closes
with the fifth customer or 90 days after opening.

## 16. Force majeure and third-party services

Neither party is liable for delay or non-performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control. Think28 is not
liable for the unavailability of third-party services (publishing platforms, stock providers, language models) and,
if one of those dependencies fails, will propose an alternative or adjust the deadline.

## 17. Notices and changes

Notices under this agreement are sent by email to the addresses on the order form. Think28 may update these terms
and its prices by publishing the new version on frame28.app; orders in progress and locked Founders prices are
