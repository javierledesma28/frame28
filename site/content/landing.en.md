## 2. Who it's for

## For brands that already shoot and never get to publish

**Brands with a product that demonstrates itself.** Kits, tools, DIY, kitchen, sports, beauty. You have tutorials
that work and no time to turn them into shorts, a cover and a product page.

**Brands selling in several countries.** Your best videos exist in one language. Your other markets watch the
original with auto-captions, or don't watch at all.

**Marketing or training teams.** You want to produce in-house, with your brand and your judgment, without hiring an
editor for every piece. That's Frame28 Team.

---

## 3. How it works

## Three steps. Nothing to learn.

**1. You send the video and the brand brief.** A YouTube link or a file. The brief is ten questions: colors, logo,
tone, what you sell, what the closing card has to say. If you have brand guidelines, that's enough.

**2. We direct it and edit it by code.** We decide what goes on screen and when: the steps, the number being said,
the callout where you point, the before and after, the social proof, the call to action. No templates: every piece is
written for that video. What sells goes on screen even with the sound off.

**3. You get a contact sheet and the package.** Review every piece at a glance, ask for one round of changes, publish.
The files are yours.

---

## 4. What you get

## What comes out of a single video

- The **edited long video** with your brand: titles, steps, figures, callouts, before and after, product card and closing.
- **Vertical shorts** (9:16) with **two different hooks** per segment, to rotate creatives without shooting again.
- A **YouTube cover** and a **1:1 version** for the product page or the marketplace listing.
- Readable **subtitles** (SRT): two lines, reading pace, no overlaps.
- **Versions in other languages** with the same timing: on-screen text and subtitles. Synthetic-voice dubbing
  arrives in the next release.

**What it isn't.** Not a captioning tool, not an automatic clip generator. Nothing to learn, no app to open. It's the
result, with editorial judgment, delivered in days.

## 8. How we do it

## What's behind it

- **Direction with judgment.** Every video is analyzed end to end: what is said, where you point, which sentence
  sells. From that we write an edit script, and every piece comes from it. No templates to fill in.
- **Your video is processed on our machines.** It isn't uploaded to any editing platform. To direct the edit we
  use language models with the transcript and the review frames, under terms that don't allow training on your
  data. The details are in the [terms](/terms).
- **Licensed material.** Stock B-roll carries a per-video commercial license, documented in the delivery. Your
  footage is yours; we don't reuse it.
- **The deliverables are yours.** Final files, subtitles and versions. No watermark, no lock-in.
- **Open engine.** The code that edits your videos is public and improves with every delivery:
  [github.com/javierledesma28/frame28](https://github.com/javierledesma28/frame28).

## 10. Questions

## Frequently asked questions

**How long does it take?** Launch, five business days from the moment we have the video and the brief. On Studio we
agree on a monthly calendar.

**What do I need to send?** The video (link or file), the ten-question brand brief and, if you have it, the logo as a
vector. Nothing else.

**Does it work with an already-edited video?** Yes. We work on produced video (with titles, cuts and music) and on
creator footage, not only on a person talking to camera. We detect the text already on screen so we never cover it.

**What about languages?** We translate on-screen text and subtitles and fit them to the original rhythm, so the German
version runs as long as the English one and the cuts land in the same place. Dubbing arrives in the next release.

**Who does the work?** Think28, Javier Ledesma's studio. The edit is generated by code; direction and review are done
by a person.

**What if I'm not convinced?** You have one round of changes on Launch and two per piece on Studio. And the guarantee:
if you don't publish half of the pieces within thirty days, we refund it.

**Can I do it myself?** Yes. With Team we install Frame28 on your team's machines, configure your brand and train you.
And if you're a technical creator, the open plugin is free.
